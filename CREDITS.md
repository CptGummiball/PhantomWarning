## Original concept: Insomnia Notifier

Phantom Warning is based on the concept of **Insomnia Notifier**.

| Versions of the original | Fabric, Minecraft 1.21.3 – 1.21.8 |

**Functional inspiration.** The original's public description defined what Phantom Warning does: a small,
client-side icon in the vanilla status-effect area that appears after a long time without using a bed, resets
after using a bed or dying, is shown only in the Overworld, adds no sounds or other HUD clutter, and needs no
server installation. Phantom Warning follows that behaviour by default and adds what the original listed as
future ideas (a separate alert state when the observable spawn conditions are met) as an option.

**Independent reimplementation.** Phantom Warning is an independent reimplementation for Minecraft 26.3 on
Fabric and NeoForge. No source code, class, file, texture or other asset of Insomnia Notifier was used,
copied or adapted; its source code was not read during development. All code, the pixel art and all texts were
written for this project. The phantom-spawn rules were taken from Minecraft 26.3 itself.

**No affiliation.** Phantom Warning and its author (cptgummiball) are not affiliated with, endorsed by or
connected to Arthur Ginzburg or the Insomnia Notifier project. Please report problems with Phantom Warning to
<https://github.com/CptGummiball/PhantomWarning/issues>, not to the original author.
